How it works

A simple rule. A satisfying challenge.

Start with a knight

It jumps in an L: two squares one way, one to the side. The squares it can reach next are marked.

Plan your route

Never land on a square twice. On the Daily Puzzle, numbered clues show where your tour must pass.

Complete the tour

Visit every square exactly once. Solve a Daily Puzzle at a level and its medal is yours.

Choose your challenge

Daily Puzzle

A new board every day — the same one for every player — at five levels from Easy to Insane. It changes at midnight where you are, and every day you solve keeps your streak going.

Classic

Free-form and Obstacle modes, on boards from Small (6×6) to Expert (12×12). Play whenever you like, at your own pace.

Daily Trivia

A new question about chess and the knight, every day. The answer is in the app.

Chronicles

Your Classic and Daily records in one place: games solved, best and average times, your streak and your medals.

Grow with every challenge
