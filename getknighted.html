How it works

A simple rule. A satisfying challenge.

Start with a knight

It jumps in an L: two squares one way, one to the side. The squares it can reach next are marked.

Plan your route

Never land on a square twice. On the Daily Puzzle, numbered clues show which move lands where, and your tour must pass through them.

Complete the tour

Visit every square exactly once. Solve a Daily Puzzle at a level and its medal is yours.

Choose your challenge

Daily Puzzle

A new board every day — the same one for every player — at five levels from Easy to Insane. It changes at midnight where you are; solve each day's puzzle on its day to keep your streak going.

♞︎

Classic

Free-form and Obstacle modes, on 6×6 and 8×8 boards, plus 10×10 and 12×12 with Premium. Play whenever you like, at your own pace.

Daily Trivia

A new question about chess and the knight, every day. The answer is in the app.
