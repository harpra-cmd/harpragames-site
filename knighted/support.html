Knighted! Help

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to support@harpragames.com and we'll get back to you.

I bought Premium. How do I get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Open Premium and tap Restore Purchase. Use the same store account you bought it with.

My progress didn't move to my new phone

Knighted! has no account and no server, so your games and stats live only on the phone you play on. A new phone starts fresh; Premium can always be restored.

Daily reminders don't arrive

Turn reminders on in Settings, then check that notifications for Knighted! are allowed in your phone's settings.

Everyone gets the same Daily puzzle?

Yes. Each date has one puzzle per level, the same for every player, and it changes at midnight where you are.
