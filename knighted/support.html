Help & Support

Need a hand? Find answers below. If you still need help, write to support@harpragames.com.

How to play

  • Knight MoveTwo squares one way, then one across — always an L.
  • ObjectiveVisit every square exactly once. Reach 64/64 on the Standard (8×8) board.
  • Valid Next MoveEvery green number is a valid next move.

Getting started

What is Knighted!?

A puzzle game built on the Knight's Tour: move a chess knight around the board and visit every square exactly once.

Which phones can play?

Knighted! is coming soon to Android phones, on Google Play, and to iPhone, on the App Store. It is free to play.

How does a knight move?

In an L-shape: two squares in one direction, then one square to the side. The squares the knight can reach next are marked on the board.

How do I complete a puzzle?

Keep jumping until the knight has landed on every square once, without landing on any square twice. On the Daily Puzzle, numbered clues show which move lands where, and your tour must pass through them.

Game modes

What is the difference between the Daily Puzzle and Classic?

The Daily Puzzle is one board a day, the same for every player, at five levels from Easy to Insane. It changes at midnight where you are.

Classic is free play, whenever you like: Free-form or Obstacle mode, on a 6×6 or 8×8 board, or 10×10 and 12×12 with Premium.

Can I play past Daily Puzzles?

Yes, from the calendar. A free player can watch a short ad to unlock more past days for 24 hours; the same goes for past trivia questions. Premium unlocks every past Daily Puzzle and trivia question for good.

How do I earn a medal?

Solve the Daily Puzzle at a level and you earn that level's medal: Squire (Easy), Cavalier (Medium), Paladin (Hard), Banneret (Expert) and Viceroy (Insane). Expert and Insane are part of Premium.

How do streaks work?

Solve the Daily Puzzle on its own day, day after day, and your streak grows. Puzzles played later from the calendar count as solved but don't mend a broken streak. Chronicles shows your current and best streak.

What is Daily Trivia?

A new question about chess and the knight every day, from the home screen. Pick an answer to see if you were right.

Hints and undo

What does Hint do?

Hint offers three kinds of help. Check your path finds where your path stops working. Guide me points you to the right next move; on the Daily Puzzle it also shows why a wrong move fails. Show me plays out the solution.

Every game comes with 2 free checks of your path. After those, and for Guide me and Show me, a short ad unlocks the help. Premium removes the ads and the limits.

What are clues and pencil marks?

On the Daily Puzzle, press and hold an empty square to pencil in a guess, or to reveal a clue. Pencil marks are free; each clue you reveal takes a short ad, and Premium reveals them without ads.

Can I undo a move?

Yes. You have 8 undos per game; after that, a short ad unlocks unlimited undos for that game. Premium has no undo limit.

Zen Mode and ads

What is Zen Mode?

A calm, distraction-free way to play: the screen clears to the board, with small Undo and Redo buttons beside it. Tap Zen Mode on the game screen to start. You get 4 free uses; a short ad gives you 4 more, and Premium has no limit.

Why are there ads, and can I remove them?

Ads keep Knighted! free. Premium removes them on every screen, for good. Where the law requires it, you can review how ads use your data in the app under Help → Privacy & Data → Review your ad privacy choices.

Progress and devices

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your phone. Your games, stats, streaks, medals and settings are kept on the device you play on. There is no account, so nothing is stored online.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

No. Progress lives only on the phone you play on, so a new phone, or reinstalling the app, starts fresh.

Premium is different: it belongs to your store account and can always be restored (below).

Premium and purchases

How do I restore Premium?

Open Premium in the app and tap Restore Purchase. Use the same store account you bought it with.

What does Premium include?

No ads, Expert and Insane, every past Daily Puzzle and trivia question, bigger boards, all eight themes, no limits on hints, undos and Zen, and offline access. It is a one-time purchase, yours forever. See everything Premium includes.

Technical help

Daily reminders don't arrive

Turn reminders on in Settings, then check that notifications for Knighted! are allowed in your phone's settings.

The game isn't behaving as expected

Close Knighted! fully and open it again, and make sure the app is up to date in your store. If ads keep failing to load, restarting the phone often helps.

Don't clear the app's data or reinstall it to fix a problem: that deletes your progress, which is stored only on your phone. Write to us first.
